ACME Talent Insights¶

Who this is for: HR leaders and managers (each section starts with the question and ends with what it means), and engineers (the code shows exactly how each number is produced).

Where the numbers come from: the Gold layer of the ACME talent data pipeline (data/talent-pipeline/job.py), which ingests HR data from PostgreSQL and learning events from S3, validates and cleans them (Silver) and builds one curated table per business question (Gold). This notebook only reads the Gold tables; it never changes data.

# Business question Gold table
1 What are the performance ratings and review history for each employee? gold_employee_performance_history
2 Which employees have critical skill gaps in key competencies? gold_skill_gaps
3 Who are the high-potential employees ready for promotion? gold_promotion_pipeline
4 What training and development has each employee completed? gold_employee_learning_summary
5 Which employees are at risk of attrition based on performance trends? gold_attrition_risk
6 How does employee performance correlate with team and project outcomes? gold_team_performance_outcomes
7 What is the distribution of skills across the organisation? gold_skill_distribution

How to run: select the kernel Python (talent-pipeline) and Run All. Connection settings come from environment variables (POSTGRES_*, LAKE_BUCKET, AWS_*); if they are not set, the notebook reads data/talent-pipeline/env.local. Every finding sentence is computed from the data, so it stays correct after the pipeline re-runs.

What are the performance ratings and review history for each employee?

Each employee has a review per half-year (2024-H2, 2025-H1, rating 1-5). The pipeline adds the previous rating, the change and a trend label for every review.

5. Attrition risk¶

Which employees are at risk of attrition based on performance trends?

An explainable score, not a black box. Each factor adds points and is listed as a reason: HR flagged High (+3) or Medium (+1), rating declined since the last review (+2), latest rating <= 2 (+1), no learning activity in 180 days (+1). High risk = 4+ points, Medium = 2-3, Low = 0-1.

Assumptions and limitations¶

Business rules (assumptions, agreed definitions to be confirmed with HR):

  • Critical skill gap = required level − proficiency ≥ 2.
  • Promotion candidate = High potential + Ready Now + latest rating ≥ 4.
  • Attrition risk points: HR High +3 / Medium +1, rating declined +2, latest rating ≤ 2 +1, no learning in 180 days +1; High ≥ 4, Medium ≥ 2.
  • "As of" date = the pipeline run date; learning activity after it is not counted.

Limitations of the data:

  • Only two review periods per employee: a trend is "better/same/worse than last time", not a long-term trajectory.
  • Only 27 of 100 teams have projects, so section 6 is indicative only.
  • Each competency is assessed for roughly 100 employees (two assessments per person), so skill distribution is a sample.
  • The learning feed is not a complete funnel (completions without enrolments).
  • The data is synthetic sample data; departments and categories look more uniform than real organisations usually do.
